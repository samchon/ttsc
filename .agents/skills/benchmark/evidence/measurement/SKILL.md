# Measurement

Everything here is the benchmark's own perspective: how a cell is run, whether what it did still counts, and what the campaign is allowed to publish about it.

A measurement is worth exactly as much as two guarantees. The arms differed in one thing only, and every number came from the retained record rather than from someone's reconstruction.

## [Running A Campaign](running.md)

Campaign start, frozen inputs, launch, port blocks, checkpoints and supervision cadence.

## [Cell Integrity](integrity.md)

The cell's permitted edits and the changes that void a measurement. Read before reporting tampering.

## [Plain Review](plain-review.md)

Review boundaries, independent inspection and retained verdicts.

## [Dashboard](dashboard.md)

The generated live record in the campaign pull-request body.

Tracked aggregate artifacts, cohort closure and pull-request completion.
